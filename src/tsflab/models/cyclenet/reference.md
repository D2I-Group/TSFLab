# CycleNet — reference

## Paper

- **Title**: CycleNet: Enhancing Time Series Forecasting through Modeling Periodic Patterns
- **Venue**: NeurIPS 2024 (Spotlight)
- **Published**: 2024 (arXiv: 2024-09)
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18479

## Abstract

Stable periodic patterns are the foundation of long-horizon forecasting. Residual Cycle Forecasting (RCF)
models them with learnable recurrent cycles and predicts the residual; combined with a Linear layer or a
shallow MLP it forms CycleNet, which reaches state-of-the-art accuracy on electricity, weather and energy
data with over 90% fewer parameters. RCF also improves existing models such as PatchTST and iTransformer
as a plug-in.
