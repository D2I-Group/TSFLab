# MegaCRN — reference

## Paper

Jiang et al., AAAI 2023 (arXiv 2211.14701, 2022-11).

To handle spatio-temporal heterogeneity and non-stationarity in traffic streams, the paper proposes Spatio-Temporal
Meta-Graph Learning, a graph structure learning mechanism realised as MegaCRN: a Meta-Graph Learner powered by a
Meta-Node Bank plugged into a GCRN encoder-decoder. It beats the state of the art on METR-LA, PEMS-BAY, and the new
EXPY-TKY (1843 Tokyo expressway links), disentangles road links and time slots with different patterns, and adapts
robustly to anomalous traffic situations.
