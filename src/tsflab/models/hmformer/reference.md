# HMformer — reference

## Paper

- **Title**: HMformer: Unleashing Transformer's Potential for Time Series Forecasting via Hierarchical Multi-Scale Modeling
- **Venue**: AAAI 2026
- **Proceedings**: https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/39355

## Abstract (shortened)

Real-world time series often exhibit complex multi-scale periodicities that the original NLP Transformer does not model well. HMformer combines a hierarchical cross-scale mixing mechanism that aggregates temporal information from fine to coarse granularities, a scale-adaptive feature expansion that enhances high-level temporal semantics, and a multi-branch complementary prediction strategy that integrates diverse temporal patterns. Experiments cover long-term and short-term forecasting benchmarks.
