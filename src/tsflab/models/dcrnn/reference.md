# DCRNN — reference

## Paper

- **Title**: Diffusion Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network: Data-Driven Traffic Forecasting
- **Venue**: ICLR 2018
- **Published**: 2018 (arXiv: 2017-07)
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.01926

## Abstract

Traffic forecasting is hard because of complex spatial dependency on road networks, non-linear temporal
dynamics, and the difficulty of long-term forecasting. DCRNN models traffic flow as a diffusion process on a
directed graph: bidirectional random walks capture spatial dependency, and an encoder-decoder with scheduled
sampling captures temporal dependency, improving 12-15% over baselines on two large road-network datasets.
