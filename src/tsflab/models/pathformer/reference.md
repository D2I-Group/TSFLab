# Pathformer — reference

## Paper

- **Title**: Pathformer: Multi-scale Transformers with Adaptive Pathways for Time Series Forecasting
- **Venue**: ICLR 2024 (arXiv 2402.05956, 2024-02)
- **Abstract (shortened)**: Forecasting Transformers usually model limited or fixed scales. Pathformer integrates temporal resolution and temporal distance: patches of several sizes divide the series, and dual attention over them captures global correlations and local details. Adaptive pathways adjust the multi-scale modelling to the input's temporal dynamics, improving accuracy and transfer generalization on eleven datasets.
