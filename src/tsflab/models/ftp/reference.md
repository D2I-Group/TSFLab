## Paper

- **Title**: Unifying Channel Independence and Mixing: Multi-Scale Patch Recursion for Global-Local Representation Synergy in Multivariate Time Series Forecasting
- **Venue**: AAAI 2026
- **Published**: 2026
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i33.40072

## Abstract

Dual-GLF runs channel-independent (CI) and channel-mixing (CM) perspectives in parallel, using multi-scale patch recursion to capture local and global temporal patterns. A Channel Enhancement (CE) module enhances salient channel features and diffuses them across channels, aiming at sensitivity to anomalies and underlying drivers. Linear fusion aggregates the three streams into a global-local representation; the paper reports competitive results on standard long-term forecasting benchmarks.
