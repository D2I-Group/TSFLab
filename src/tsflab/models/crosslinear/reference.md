# CrossLinear — reference

## Paper

- **Title**: CrossLinear: Plug-and-Play Cross-Correlation Embedding for Time Series Forecasting with Exogenous Variables
- **Venue**: KDD 2025
- **Published**: 2025 (arXiv: 2025-05)
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23116

## Abstract

Forecasting with exogenous variables requires telling endogenous and exogenous variables apart, and
traditional models overfit dependency modelling. CrossLinear adds a lightweight plug-and-play
cross-correlation embedding that captures only time-invariant, direct variable dependencies, plus
patch-wise processing and a global linear head for short- and long-term dependencies. It reports superior
short- and long-term results on 12 real-world datasets.
