# Amplifier — reference

## Paper

- **Title**: Amplifier: Bringing Attention to Neglected Low-Energy Components in Time Series Forecasting
- **Venue**: AAAI 2025 (arXiv 2501.17216, 2025-01)
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17216

## Abstract

Existing forecasters easily overlook low-energy components. An energy amplification block raises their energy to improve learning, and an energy restoration block returns it to the original level.
Because amplified data shows two distinct spectral energy peaks, the technique is combined with a seasonal-trend forecaster that models the two peaks independently.
A semi-channel interaction block captures temporal relations from channel commonality and specificity; experiments on eight benchmarks show gains in effectiveness and efficiency.
