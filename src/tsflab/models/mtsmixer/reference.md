# MTSMixer — reference

## Paper

- **Title**: MTS-Mixers: Multivariate Time Series Forecasting via Factorized Temporal and Channel Mixing
- **Venue**: IJCNN 2025 (arXiv 2302.04501, 2023-02)
- **Abstract (shortened)**: Studies the role of attention in Transformer forecasters and finds that (1) attention is not necessary for capturing temporal dependencies, (2) entangled and redundant capture of temporal and channel interaction hurts accuracy, and (3) the mapping between input and prediction sequence matters. MTS-Mixers use two factorized modules for temporal and channel dependence and outperform Transformer models with higher efficiency.
